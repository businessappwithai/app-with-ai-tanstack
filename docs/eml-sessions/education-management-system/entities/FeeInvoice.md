# FeeInvoice  ·  standalone  ·  category: Finance

**Role.** What a student's guardians owe for one term. Raised at the census date, so a student enrolled that day is billed whether or not they attend.

**Placement.** Standalone. The finance office works a list of invoices.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `invoice_number` | string | UK | The number quoted on every statement and payment, e.g. INV-2026-AUT-0331. Unique, sequential within a term, and never reused even when an invoice is cancelled. |
| `student_id` | string | FK | The student the fees are for. The invoice goes to their primary contact guardian, which is why that flag on the guardian link is a finance field as much as a safeguarding one. |
| `academic_term_id` | string | FK | The term being billed. One invoice per student per term; an extra charge later in the term is a line on the same invoice, not a second invoice. |
| `status` | string | — | draft — being assembled, nothing sent; issued — sent to the guardian and payable; part_paid — some money received, a balance outstanding; paid — settled in full; overdue — past the due date with a balance, which starts the finance office's chase; cancelled — written off or raised in error, and the reason is required. |
| `issued_on` | date | OPTIONAL | When the invoice was sent. Blank while in draft; the due date and every reminder are measured from it. |
| `due_on` | date | OPTIONAL | When payment is expected. Thirty days from issue by school policy, and the overdue report reads nothing else. |
| `total_amount` | decimal | — | The sum of the invoice's lines, gross, in school currency. Recalculated by the invoice hooks whenever a line changes, and frozen once the invoice is issued. |
| `amount_paid` | decimal | — | How much has been received against this invoice so far. Maintained by the payment hooks, never keyed by hand — a manual adjustment here is how an invoice comes to disagree with its own payments. |
| `balance_due` | decimal | — | What is still owed: total less payments. The payment rule reads this to refuse an overpayment, and the finance dashboard sorts by it. |
| `cancellation_reason` | text | OPTIONAL | Why the invoice was cancelled — bursary awarded, raised in error, place never taken. Required on cancellation, because a cancelled invoice is money the school chose not to collect and every one of them is looked at. |

## Rules

### `invoiceControl` on `beforeUpdate` — **`%%action` decision table — it can act**

- `requireInvoiceCancellationReason validation-error when: status == "cancelled" and cancellation_reason == null message: A cancelled invoice is money the school chose not to collect — every one of them needs a reason.`
- `requireIssueDate validation-error when: status == "issued" and issued_on == null message: An issued invoice needs its issue date — the due date and every reminder are measured from it.`

## Cross-entity effects

Its totals are written by the `FeeInvoiceLine` and `Payment` hooks, never keyed; its status is moved by the two payment sagas. `mark_overdue` is a scheduled move rather than a rule — a rule cannot read the clock — and it is called out here so nobody expects the transition to fire itself.

Sagas that touch it:

- **AdmissionToEnrolment**
  - `B CreateEntity entity: Student as: newStudentId fields: {"first_name":"applicant_first_name","last_name":"applicant_last_name","date_of_birth":"applicant_date_of_birth","year_group":"year_group_applied_for","status":"enrolled","has_support_plan":false}`
  - `C UpdateEntity field: student_id source: newStudentId`
  - `D CreateEntity entity: FeeInvoice as: newInvoiceId fields: {"student_id":"newStudentId","academic_term_id":"academic_term_id","status":"draft","total_amount":0,"amount_paid":0,"balance_due":0}`

## Open questions

Instalment plans. A plan is a schedule of expected payments, which this model does not carry; today a plan is an understanding between the office and a family, and `finance-debt-ageing` is what shows whether it is being kept.
