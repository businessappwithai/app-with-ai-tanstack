# StudentGuardian  ·  child of Student

**Role.** One guardian's relationship to one student — the kind of relationship, the contact order and whether they may collect. This is where the duty of care is recorded.

**Placement.** Child of `Student` (§3.3.1: Q1 no — a list of links away from the child is not a screen; Q2 and Q3 yes). It appears as a tab on the student record, which is where the office looks for it.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `student_id` | string | FK | The child. This is the link back to the parent record, so guardians appear as a tab on the student rather than as a screen of their own. |
| `guardian_id` | string | FK | The adult. One guardian may be linked to several students, which is why this is a link record rather than a column on the student. |
| `relationship` | string | — | mother, father, step_parent, grandparent, carer — the relationship as the family states it; local_authority — a corporate parent, which changes who may consent; other — anything else, and the detail belongs in the student's pastoral record. |
| `is_primary_contact` | boolean | — | Exactly one guardian per student carries this. They receive the invoice, the reports and the first phone call, and setting it on a second guardian is what the primary-contact exception report exists to catch. |
| `may_collect` | boolean | — | Whether this adult may take the child from the premises. Cleared where a court order says otherwise, and the reception screen reads nothing else. |
| `contact_order` | integer | — | The order the school works down in an emergency, lowest first. The primary contact is normally 1, but a guardian who works away may deliberately not be. |

## Cross-entity effects

None written automatically. The rule that exactly one guardian per student carries `is_primary_contact` is not enforceable by a rule that can only read the record being written — a second primary contact is a two-row fact — so it is a report, and named as such rather than promised as a refusal.
