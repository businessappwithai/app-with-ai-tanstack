# ClassSection  ·  standalone  ·  category: Academics

**Role.** One taught group of a course in one term, with a teacher, a room and a capacity. The thing a student is actually enrolled in.

**Placement.** Standalone. The timetable is a list of sections and it is opened daily.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `code` | string | UK | The timetable code, e.g. MATH-H-A-2026AUT. Unique — it is how a teacher, a room and a group are matched on a printed timetable. |
| `course_id` | string | FK | The subject being taught. Fixed at timetabling: moving a section to a different course is a cancellation and a new section, because the students enrolled chose the subject. |
| `academic_term_id` | string | FK | The term this group runs in. A section belongs to exactly one term, which is what makes a term's results a closed set. |
| `staff_id` | string | FK | The teacher who takes the group and marks its work. Named staff_id rather than teacher_id so it resolves to the Staff table and renders as a person picker rather than a raw id. |
| `room_id` | string | FK OPTIONAL | The room the group normally meets in. Optional for a section taught off-site or online, and a session may override it for one meeting. |
| `capacity` | integer | — | Places in this group. Seeded from the room's capacity and lowered where the subject needs bench space; never raised above the room's, and the enrolment handler refuses a place past it. |
| `enrolled_count` | integer | — | How many students currently hold a place. Maintained by the enrolment hooks rather than counted on every screen, because the timetable shows fill on every row and the count would otherwise be a query per row. |
| `day_of_week` | string | — | The teaching day the group meets. One day and one period per section keeps the timetable a grid a person can read; a subject needing two slots is two sections of the same course. |
| `period` | integer | — | The numbered teaching period within the day, 1 to 8. With day_of_week it is the clash key: a student may not hold two enrolments in the same day and period. |
| `status` | string | — | planned — being timetabled, not yet open for enrolment; open — accepting students; in_progress — teaching, enrolment closed to newcomers; completed — the term ended and final grades are in; cancelled — the group will not run, which withdraws every enrolment on it. |
| `cancellation_reason` | text | OPTIONAL | Why the group was pulled — under-recruitment, no teacher, room lost. Shown to every affected family in the notice, so it is written for them rather than for the office. |

## Hooks

None.

## Cross-entity effects

Cancelling a section withdraws every enrolment on it — trigger `ClassSection` → target `Enrollment` → transition `pending|enrolled → withdrawn` — through `SectionCancellation`. The enrolment counter is maintained by the `Enrollment` hooks rather than by this entity, and `registrar-counter-disagrees` is the report that notices when a hook fails silently.

Sagas that touch it:

- **SectionCancellation** *(triggered on this entity)*
  - `B UpdateEntity entity: Enrollment targetField: class_section_id field: status value: withdrawn`

## Open questions

One day and one period per section keeps the timetable readable; a subject needing two slots a week is modelled as two sections of the same course. Confirmed as a deliberate simplification, and the one most likely to need revisiting in a real school.
