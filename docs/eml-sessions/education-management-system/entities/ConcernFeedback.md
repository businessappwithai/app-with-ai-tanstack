# ConcernFeedback  ·  standalone  ·  category: Student Support

**Role.** What a member of staff who reported a safeguarding concern is told about it afterwards: that it was received, triaged, acted on, referred or closed. It carries the case reference and an outcome in neutral words and never the student, the account or the risk, so a teacher who raised a concern can read it back without being able to read the concern.

**Placement.** Standalone. A reporter opens a list of what became of the concerns they raised, away from any one student or concern; it is not a child of `SafeguardingConcern`, because a child inherits its parent's readers and the whole point is a different, wider set.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `safeguarding_concern_id` | string | FK | The concern this feedback answers. The feedback names it for the designated staff; it is not a way for the reader to open it. |
| `reported_by_id` | string | FK | The member of staff who reported the concern and is being told about it, copied from the concern by the feedback hook. |
| `reference` | string | — | The concern's case reference, copied onto the feedback so a reporter can match it to the note they made. The reference carries no name, so it can be read by staff who may not read the concern. |
| `outcome` | string | — | received — the designated lead has the concern; triaged — risk and an owner are assigned; action_taken — the school is acting on it; referred — passed to an external body; closed — nothing further remains with the school. |
| `given_at` | datetime | — | When the reporter was told. It is stamped as the concern changes state, so the reporter can see the school responded promptly. |
| `message` | text | — | A short neutral note for the reporter, such as that the concern was referred and they need do nothing more. It must not contain the student's name, what was alleged or the assessed risk. |

## Cross-entity effects

Written by the `SafeguardingConcern` hooks (`acknowledgeConcernToReporter`, `reportOutcomeToReporter`), never keyed by the reporter. It copies `reported_by_id` and the case `reference` from the concern and holds no student, no account and no risk level.

## Open questions

`%%rbac` cannot scope rows, so a `teacher` can read every feedback row, not only their own; a row exposes a case reference, an outcome category and a neutral message and nothing that identifies a child. If row scoping arrives, filter on `reported_by_id`. Whether a reporter should be told at every status change or only at the end is a school policy this model fixes at every change.
