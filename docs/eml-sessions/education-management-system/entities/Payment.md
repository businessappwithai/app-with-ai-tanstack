# Payment  ·  standalone  ·  category: Finance

**Role.** Money received against an invoice. Recorded once and never edited; a correction is a second, offsetting record.

**Placement.** Standalone. The daily takings list is payments across invoices.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `fee_invoice_id` | string | FK | The invoice this money settles. A payment always names an invoice; money received with no invoice is held by the finance office outside this system until one exists. |
| `received_by_id` | string | FK OPTIONAL | The member of staff who took the payment. Blank for an automated bank feed, and its blankness is what separates counter takings from transfers in the daily reconciliation. |
| `amount` | decimal | — | The sum received, gross, in school currency. Never edited — a correction is a second, offsetting record, so the audit trail survives. |
| `balance_before` | decimal | — | What the invoice still owed immediately before this payment was applied, stamped by the payment hook. It is on the receipt for the guardian's benefit, and it is what the overpayment rule compares against — the rule can only read the record being written, so the balance has to be on it. |
| `method` | string | — | card — taken at the office or online; bank_transfer — matched against the statement, so it may be recorded days after it arrived; cash — counted into the safe and reconciled the same day; cheque — cleared before the invoice is credited; bursary — an internal award rather than money, and it settles the invoice without any cash moving. |
| `received_at` | datetime | — | When the money actually arrived, not when it was keyed in. Transfers are routinely recorded late, and the reconciliation depends on the distinction. |
| `reference` | string | OPTIONAL | The bank reference, card authorisation or cheque number used to match this payment against the statement. Optional for cash, where there is nothing to match. |

## Enums

- `method` → **PaymentMethod**: card, bank_transfer, cash, cheque, bursary

## Lifecycle

None. This entity has no status column and no lifecycle.

## Rules

### `paymentAcceptance` on `beforeCreate` — **`%%action` decision table — it can act**

- `refuseOverpayment validation-error when: amount > balance_before message: A payment cannot exceed what the invoice still owes. A credit is a decision for the finance office, not a keying accident.`
- `refuseNonPositivePayment validation-error when: amount <= 0 message: A payment must be a positive sum. A correction is a second, offsetting record.`
- `settleInvoice trigger-workflow when: amount >= balance_before workflow: InvoiceSettlement`
- `postPartPayment trigger-workflow when: amount < balance_before workflow: PartPaymentPosting`

## Hooks

- `beforeCreate` → `resolveInvoiceBalance`
- `afterCreate` → `applyPaymentToInvoice`

## Cross-entity effects

`resolveInvoiceBalance` stamps `balance_before` before the write, `applyPaymentToInvoice` applies the money after it, and the rule then moves the invoice through `InvoiceSettlement` or `PartPaymentPosting`. That order is the §10.4 pattern stated exactly: a handler resolves, the rule decides and acts.

Sagas that touch it:

- **InvoiceSettlement** *(triggered on this entity)*
  - `B UpdateEntity entity: FeeInvoice targetField: fee_invoice_id field: status value: paid`
- **PartPaymentPosting** *(triggered on this entity)*
  - `B UpdateEntity entity: FeeInvoice targetField: fee_invoice_id field: status value: part_paid`

## Open questions

Refunds. A negative payment is refused by `refuseNonPositivePayment`, so a refund today is a cancelled invoice and a re-issue. A real refund record was ruled out of scope at Gate B.
