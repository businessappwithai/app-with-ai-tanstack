# Staff  ·  standalone  ·  category: People

**Role.** An employee of the school, teaching or administrative. This is the model's person entity, so every _by_id column in the model resolves to a member of staff.

**Placement.** Standalone, and the model's person entity: every `_by_id` column in the document resolves here because no `User` entity is declared.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `staff_number` | string | UK | The payroll identifier, unique and unchanging. It is how a member of staff is matched against the HR system this application deliberately does not hold. |
| `email` | email | UK | The school address used for the teaching rota, marking reminders and password recovery. Unique — a second appointment reuses the existing record rather than creating a rival one. |
| `first_name` | string | — | Given name, as shown on the staff list and in every person picker in the application. |
| `last_name` | string | — | Family name. The staff list sorts by it, which is why the name is stored in two fields rather than one. |
| `department_id` | string | FK OPTIONAL | The department a teacher belongs to. Optional because administrative and leadership staff belong to none, and a blank here is what keeps them out of departmental marking reports. |
| `job_title` | string | — | The title as it appears in the school's own directory, e.g. "Teacher of Physics". Free text — it is written on letters, not reported on. |
| `staff_type` | string | — | teaching — takes sections and marks work; administrative — office and finance; pastoral — counselling and conduct; leadership — the senior team. This decides which reports a person is offered, so it is not a job description. |
| `status` | string | — | active — may be timetabled; on_leave — keeps their sections but cannot be assigned new ones; left — retained for the marking and conduct trail, never assignable. |
| `started_on` | date | — | The date the appointment began. Used for length-of-service reporting and never changed by a change of role. |
| `phone` | phone | OPTIONAL | A direct number, used only for same-day cover arrangements where email is too slow. Optional. |

## Cross-entity effects

A member of staff going `on_leave` or `left` leaves their sections pointing at them; the `principal-teaching-cover-gap` report is what surfaces it. Deliberately not automated — reassigning a group mid-term is a timetabling decision, not a side effect of an HR field.
