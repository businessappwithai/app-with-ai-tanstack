# DisciplineIncident  ·  standalone  ·  category: Conduct

**Role.** A recorded conduct event, its investigation and its outcome. Readable by pastoral staff and the principal only.

**Placement.** Standalone. The pastoral team works a list of incidents.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `reference` | string | UK | The reference the incident is known by in pastoral meetings and in any correspondence with the family, e.g. INC-2026-0087. Unique. |
| `student_id` | string | FK | The student the incident concerns. One record per student per incident — an incident involving three students is three records, so that each family's correspondence stays separate. |
| `reported_by_id` | string | FK | The member of staff who reported it. Named reported_by_id so it resolves to the Staff table, and it is never blank: an anonymous conduct record cannot be investigated. |
| `occurred_at` | datetime | — | When the incident happened, not when it was written up. The gap between the two is what the reporting-delay report measures, and a long one is a safeguarding finding in itself. |
| `category` | string | — | disruption — classroom behaviour; absence_without_leave — off site without permission; bullying — treated as safeguarding-adjacent and never closed without a recorded outcome; damage — property, and a fee line may follow; academic_misconduct — plagiarism or examination irregularity, which the examination board may need to be told about; safeguarding — child protection, which restricts who may read the record further. |
| `severity` | string | — | minor — dealt with in the room; moderate — a sanction and a note home; serious — a meeting with the family; critical — exclusion is on the table and the principal is involved from the start. |
| `status` | string | — | reported — written up, nobody assigned; under_review — being investigated by pastoral staff; action_taken — a sanction has been decided and applied; closed — the matter is finished and the outcome recorded; dismissed — investigated and found not to have happened, which is a finding and not a deletion. |
| `summary` | text | — | What happened, factually, in the words of the person who saw it. Written knowing the family may read it, because in a serious case they will. |
| `sanction` | string | OPTIONAL | none — recorded, no sanction; warning — spoken or written; detention — supervised time; parental_meeting — the family is asked in; suspension — the student is barred from sessions and their status changes; exclusion — permanent, and the governors are involved. Required before an incident can move to action_taken. |
| `sanction_notes` | text | OPTIONAL | The detail of the sanction — how long, when, who supervised. The field the appeal is answered from, and the reason a bare enum was not enough. |
| `closed_on` | date | OPTIONAL | When the matter was finished. The open-incidents report is everything with no date here, and the ageing on that list is the pastoral team's real workload. |

## Rules

### `conductOutcome` on `beforeUpdate` — **`%%action` decision table — it can act**

- `requireSanction validation-error when: status == "action_taken" and sanction == null message: An incident cannot move to action taken with no sanction recorded — including a sanction of none, which is itself a decision.`
- `requireSanctionNotes validation-error when: sanction == "suspension" and sanction_notes == null message: A suspension needs its detail — how long, from when, and who agreed it. The appeal is answered from this field.`
- `requireClosureDate validation-error when: status == "closed" and closed_on == null message: A closed incident needs the date it was closed; the ageing on the open list is measured from it.`
- `suspendOnSuspension trigger-workflow when: sanction == "suspension" workflow: SuspensionEscalation`

## Cross-entity effects

A suspension sanction suspends the student — trigger `DisciplineIncident` → target `Student` → transition `enrolled|on_leave → suspended` — through `SuspensionEscalation`. Nothing lifts the suspension automatically: `reinstate` is a person's decision, and `counsellor-current-suspensions` is the list it is made from.

Sagas that touch it:

- **SuspensionEscalation** *(triggered on this entity)*
  - `B UpdateEntity entity: Student targetField: student_id field: status value: suspended`

## Open questions

Read access is `counsellor` and `principal` only, so a teacher cannot record an incident — they report it to the pastoral team, who record it with the teacher named in `reported_by_id`. The alternative (teachers create and read every incident) was rejected against research §7's safeguarding line. Row-level scoping — a teacher seeing only incidents they reported — is an application concern that `%%rbac` cannot express, and it is the change to make if the school wants teachers to record directly.
