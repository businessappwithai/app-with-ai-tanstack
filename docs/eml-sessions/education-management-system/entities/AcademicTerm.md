# AcademicTerm  ·  standalone  ·  category: Academics

**Role.** A named teaching period inside an academic year. Every enrolment, invoice and result in the school is bounded by exactly one term.

**Placement.** Standalone. Almost every screen in the application is filtered by term, so it is a first-class list.

## Fields

| Column | Type | Modifiers | Purpose |
|---|---|---|---|
| `id` | string | PK | The record's own identifier. Generated by the application and never keyed; it is never shown as a display value. |
| `code` | string | UK | The short key used in every reference number, e.g. 2026-AUT. Unique, and stable once the calendar is published. |
| `name` | string | — | The term as the school says it, e.g. "Autumn 2026". |
| `academic_year` | string | — | The year the term belongs to, e.g. "2026/2027". A label rather than a table: the school reports by year but never manages one as a record. |
| `starts_on` | date | — | First teaching day. Sessions may not be scheduled before it. |
| `ends_on` | date | — | Last teaching day. Results for the term are expected within ten working days of it. |
| `census_on` | date | — | The date enrolment is counted for billing. A student enrolled on this date is invoiced for the whole term whether or not they attend, so it is the date the finance office plans around. |
| `status` | string | — | planned — timetabling in progress, nothing billed; current — teaching, registers open; closed — results published and invoices final, no further writes expected. |

## Cross-entity effects

Closing a term is expected to close its sections and finalise its invoices. Neither is automated: both are decisions with money attached, and the closing checklist is the finance officer's `finance-drafts-past-census` report rather than a saga nobody watched run.
